Participation, not university admission

Admissions & Aid

This page concerns participation in SIGNAL Lab. Formal admission to Qassim University or a degree program is a separate institutional process.

Undergraduate / Senior Design

  • Relevant engineering/computing enrollment and project-specific preparation.
  • Short CV, unofficial transcript, interests, skills, and expected weekly availability.
  • For senior design, identify the type of project you are interested in.

Expected lab practices

Consistent weekly work, meetings, Git/version control, experiment documentation, reproducibility, technical writing, safety, privacy/confidentiality, and responsible AI/research ethics.

See Senior Design Projects

Graduate research

Formal university/program admission is separate from research participation or preliminary supervisor discussions.

  • CV and transcript.
  • Concise research statement and research-area preference.
  • Representative publications, code, or projects when available.
  • Expected research timeline.

Funding, assistantships, or project support depend on available projects, budgets, eligibility, and formal university approval and are not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed in writing.

Apply to participate

Submission is not an offer of admission, funding, employment, supervision, or project placement.
