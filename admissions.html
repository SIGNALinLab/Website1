Admissions & Aid

This page concerns participation in SIGNAL Lab. Formal admission to Qassim University or a degree program is separate.

Participation pathways

What to prepare

Preparation depends on the project. The guidance below is a baseline for research-participation enquiries and does not replace university admission requirements.

Undergraduate / Senior Design

Applicants should be enrolled in a relevant engineering or computing discipline and have preparation appropriate to the project.

Typical materials

  • Short CV
  • Unofficial transcript
  • Areas of research interest
  • Relevant technical skills
  • Senior-design or project preference, where applicable
  • Expected weekly availability

Project-specific preparation may include signals and systems, probability, electronics, communications, computer vision, programming, or machine learning.

See Senior Design Projects

Graduate research

Formal university/program admission is separate from preliminary research-participation or supervisor discussions.

Typical materials

  • CV
  • Transcript
  • Concise research statement
  • Research-area preference
  • Representative publications, code, or projects when available
  • Proposed or expected research timeline

Funding, assistantships, or project support depend on available projects, budgets, eligibility, and formal university approval.

Lab practice

Expected research practices

  • Consistent weekly work and scheduled project meetings
  • Git/version control and experiment documentation
  • Reproducible implementation and responsible data management
  • Technical writing and publication-quality reporting where applicable
  • Appropriate laboratory safety, privacy, confidentiality, and responsible-AI/research-ethics practices
Important distinction

Admission and funding

Participation in SIGNAL Lab does not itself constitute formal admission to Qassim University or a degree program.

Funding is not guaranteed. Funding, assistantships, or project support depend on available projects, budgets, eligibility, and formal university approval and are not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed in writing.

Apply to participate

Submission is not an offer of admission, funding, employment, supervision, or project placement.
