Participation, not university admission

Admissions & Aid

This page concerns participation in SIGNAL Lab. Formal admission to Qassim University or a degree program is separate.

Undergraduate / Senior Design

  • Short CV and unofficial transcript.
  • Areas of research interest and relevant technical skills.
  • Senior-design/project preference where applicable.
  • Expected weekly availability.

Project-specific preparation may include signals and systems, probability, electronics, communications, computer vision, programming, or machine learning.

Graduate research

  • CV and transcript.
  • Concise research statement and research-area preference.
  • Representative publications, code, or projects when available.
  • Expected research timeline.

Funding, assistantships, or project support depend on available projects, budgets, eligibility, and formal university approval and are not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed in writing.

Lab practice

Expected research practices

Consistent weekly work, scheduled meetings, Git/version control, experiment documentation, reproducibility, technical writing, laboratory safety, privacy/confidentiality, and responsible AI/research ethics.

Important distinction

Admission and funding

Participation in SIGNAL Lab does not itself constitute formal admission to Qassim University or a degree program.

Apply to participate

Submission is not an offer of admission, funding, employment, supervision, or project placement.
