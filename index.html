Research positioning

Signals into engineered intelligence

The lab’s established interests span signal and image processing and machine learning, intelligent systems, communication systems, computer vision, and engineering applications of artificial intelligence.

SIGNAL in Medicine

Biomedical signal and image research, including neuroimaging and health-oriented machine learning.

SIGNAL in Defense & Security

Biometrics, sensing, access-control research, and security-oriented computer vision without implying sponsorship or classified work.

SIGNAL in Education

Automation and intelligent systems supporting learning environments and educational workflows.

SIGNAL in Energy & Sustainability

Forecasting, scheduling, smart-grid intelligence, and renewable-energy applications.

SIGNAL in AI & Optimization

Machine learning, optimization, signal/image algorithms, and enabling intelligent-system methods.
