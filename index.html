Research domains

Signals translated into real-world engineering

Current projects and publications span five application-oriented research families.

SIGNAL in Medicine

Biomedical signal analysis, neuroimaging, EEG, and AI-assisted health classification.

SIGNAL in Defense & Security

Radar tracking, biometrics, anti-spoofing, secure access, and security-oriented sensing.

SIGNAL in Education

Computer vision and automation for educational workflows, including attendance systems.

SIGNAL in Energy & Sustainability

Solar forecasting, smart-grid scheduling, and intelligent energy-management research.

SIGNAL in AI & Optimization

Machine learning, multimodal models, metaheuristics, and engineering optimization.
